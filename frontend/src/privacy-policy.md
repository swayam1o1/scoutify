# Scoutify Privacy Policy
**Effective date:** 21 October 2026
**Last updated:** 21 October 2026

Scoutify Technologies Private Limited ("Scoutify", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular activity, we will seek that consent separately.

## 2. Information We Collect
The information we collect depends on how you interact with Scoutify and which features you use.

### 2.1 Information You Provide
We may collect information that you voluntarily submit, including:

- **Contact information:** Name, email address, phone number, and other contact details you choose to provide.
- **Professional information:** Business name, professional role, company details, areas of expertise, services offered, categories, location, and service areas.
- **Portfolio information:** Project descriptions, photographs, work samples, business descriptions, and other materials you upload or submit.
- **Early-access information:** Your name, work email, professional category, and any additional information you provide when registering your interest in Scoutify.
- **Project information:** Project names, locations, budgets, preferred materials, styles, requirements, notes, and other details you provide when using project-related features.
- **Communications:** Messages, feedback, support requests, and correspondence with our team.
Please do not submit sensitive personal information or confidential project information unless it is necessary for the feature you are using and you are comfortable sharing it.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
When you visit our website, we or our service providers may collect certain technical information, such as:

- IP address and approximate location derived from technical information.
- Browser type, device type, operating system, and language settings.
- Pages visited, referring pages, interactions, and timestamps.
- Diagnostic information, error logs, and website performance data.
- Cookie identifiers and similar technologies, where used.
The information collected depends on the technologies and analytics tools actually enabled on our website.

### 2.3 Information From Public or Third-Party Sources
Where appropriate, we may obtain professional or business information from publicly available sources, business websites, professional directories, or information provided by vendors and other partners.

We may use such information to support business discovery and maintain relevant professional listings.

We do not guarantee that information obtained from public or third-party sources is complete, current, or accurate.

### 2.4 Information From Other Users
A professional, business, or other user may submit information about their business or provide contact details for a professional connection.

If you submit information about another person, you are responsible for ensuring that you have the authority or other lawful basis required to share that information.

We may use collected information to:

1. Operate, maintain, and improve the Scoutify website.
2. Respond to early-access requests and communicate about product updates or launches.
3. Create, display, and manage professional or business profiles.
4. Organise vendors and professionals by categories, capabilities, materials, locations, and service areas.
5. Support search, discovery, shortlisting, and project-management features as they become available.
6. Respond to enquiries, provide support, and communicate with users.
7. Detect spam, fraud, unauthorised access, misuse, and security incidents.
8. Analyse website performance and understand how users interact with the platform.
9. Enforce our Terms of Service and protect our legal rights.
10. Comply with applicable laws, lawful requests, and regulatory obligations.
We will not use personal information for purposes that are incompatible with the purposes described in this Policy unless we obtain any additional consent or establish another lawful basis required by applicable law.

## 5. Public Professional Profiles and User Content
Scoutify is designed to help people discover relevant businesses and professionals.

If you submit information for a public professional profile, you understand that information designated for public display may be visible to other website visitors. Depending on the features available, this may include:

- Your name or business name.
- Professional role and business description.
- Categories, capabilities, and service areas.
- Portfolio images and project descriptions.
- Business contact details that you choose to publish.
Please do not include private phone numbers, personal email addresses, residential addresses, or confidential client information in public content unless you intend to make that information public.

You should have the necessary rights and permissions to upload photographs, project details, and other materials, including any required permission from clients, photographers, and identifiable individuals.

We may remove or restrict access to content where reasonably necessary to address complaints, protect privacy, enforce our terms, or comply with applicable law.

## 6. Project Information and Saved Collections
Scoutify may offer features that allow users to save vendors, create collections, or organise project information.

Where these features are available, information you enter may be used to provide and improve those features.

Information intended to remain private will not be deliberately published as public profile content merely because you submit it to a project workspace. However, you should review the privacy and sharing settings of any feature before entering confidential information.

If project boards or collections support sharing, the information made available to other people will depend on the sharing settings and actions you choose.
